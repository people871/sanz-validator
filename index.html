About the validator

Sanz Validator is an independent validator operated by Sanz (@Sanz2p) on the LUXA blockchain (chain-id luxa-1). The validator runs the official luxad node software and is operated directly by its owner — keys are held by the operator, never by a third party.

Our goal is simple: help secure the LUXA network with honest, transparent validation, and share in its growth with the community that delegates to us.

Security first

Validator keys are held by the operator only. No custodial setup, no shared secrets.

Transparent

Validator address, consensus key and node software are published openly on this page.

Community-driven

Built by a community member, for the community — questions and delegations are welcome any time.

How to delegate

  1. Hold LUXA on the luxa-1 network in a wallet you control.
  2. Find Sanz-Validator (luxa1nyhe…52df) in your wallet's staking / delegation list, or delegate directly to the address above.
  3. Your stake stays in your control — delegation never moves your tokens to the validator.
  4. Rewards accrue per the network's rules. Commission will be published here before launch.
